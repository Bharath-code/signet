Thesis

A signet is a ring that presses a mark into wax. The page should feel like that act: a clean impression of your brand, made in one motion. Every animation on the page is a step in that press: read, lift, set, seal. Nothing moves for decoration.

The current landing already has the right system (bone, ink, one vermilion stamp, zero radius). It reads as a well-set document. What it lacks is the moment of proof in motion: a visitor has to imagine what "reads your live site" means before they paste a URL. This spec adds one orchestrated hero sequence that shows the pipeline, and five small moments that each carry one fact. It also turns the loading wait, the part of the flow most likely to lose a visitor, into part of the show.

Motion system

Four durations and two curves cover every case. Hover a column to feel the duration. The press curve is the one already in DESIGN.md; the ink curve is new and used only for things that draw or fill, so the eye learns that "drawing" and "moving" are different acts.

--dur-tap120ms · press
Button press, active state, toggles.
--dur-ui240ms · press
Hover, arrow nudge, chevron, focus.
--dur-reveal480ms · press
Section enter, preview swap, 60ms stagger.
--dur-scene900ms · ink
Registration snap, rule draw, roller fill.

Rules

1. One orchestrated moment per page (The Press). Everything else responds to the visitor.
2. Motion carries a fact. If you can't say what the animation tells the visitor, cut it.
3. Only transform, opacity, stroke-dashoffset. Never animate layout.
4. Typing uses steps(): mechanical, like a typewriter. No bounce or elastic curves. The seal is the only overshoot, because a stamp physically does that.
5. Loops pause when off-screen and stop for good once the visitor starts typing.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every loop renders its final frame: the finished signature, the drawn rail, the registered team, the sealed envelope. The default CSS state is the final state and keyframes only add the in-between, so no JavaScript is needed to honour the setting. The Inking log still updates its text; only the fills stop animating.

4.1 The Press

Hero · the one orchestrated moment
Plate 1 · 9s loop · CSS only
  1. 01Paste the URL
  2. 02Read the live site
  3. 03Lift logo, palette, type
  4. 04Set the signature
  5. 05Seal, ready to copy
Why

It answers "what does this actually do?" before the visitor reads a word, and it shows the three things Signet extracts as physical objects moving from one place to another. The fictional Northwind brand uses a serif and a green/amber palette on purpose: the output must look nothing like Signet's own ink and vermilion, or visitors will think every signature comes out looking like us.

TriggerPlays on load as an attract loop. Stops on the first focus of the URL field and freezes on its final frame.
Live modeOn submit, replays once with the visitor's domain typed in. When the kit returns, chips take the real logo URL and hex values and land in the real previews.
A11yOne role="img" with a sentence title. Captions are real text. Reduced motion shows the sealed card.
BuildPressStage.tsx, inline SVG, keyframes in globals.css. Aspect-ratio box 760:380 so CLS is 0.

4.2 The URL bar

Hero · the one input
Plate 2 · live, try itTab to focus
Why

The placeholder types real-looking domains (northwind.co, halcyon.studio, fernbank-legal.com) in steps() rhythm. It suggests "any company works" without a sentence of copy. It stops the moment the field gets focus so it never competes with the visitor's own typing.

Typing70ms per character, 1.6s hold, 30ms per character delete. Pauses when off-screen.
ButtonsRest: flat. Hover: lift 3px up-left with a 3px ink hard shadow. Active: drop flat (120ms). The stamp you feel.
PasteOn paste, strip https:// and paths, then auto-submit after 400ms unless the visitor keeps typing.
A11yPlaceholder is decoration, the aria-label is the name. Reduced motion: static "yourcompany.com".

4.3 Process rail

Replaces the 4-card step grid
Plate 3 · draws once on enter
Why

Four identical cards with a number, a heading, and a paragraph is the template look PRODUCT.md rules out. A single drawn line says "this is one continuous act" and the nib shows where you are. Four words replace four paragraphs; the longer copy moves to the FAQ.

TriggerIntersectionObserver at 40% visible. Plays once, then rests drawn. No loop on the live page.
TimingLine draws over 3.6s on the ink curve. Each node border turns vermilion as the nib arrives.

Why

In print, misregistration is when the colour plates don't line up: the image doubles and blurs. That is exactly what a team's signatures look like, and the crop-mark crosshairs make the metaphor legible even to someone who has never seen a press. The snap into register is the product promise in one gesture: one source, every person, same mark.

TriggerEnters in drift. Snaps into register 1.2s after 50% visible, once. Button toggles both ways after that.
Timing900ms press curve, rows staggered 70ms top to bottom. Fonts swap instantly mid-move; the stagger hides the jump.
A11yState change is announced in the aria-live line. Meaning never rests on colour alone: each row also labels its fault.
BuildRegistration.tsx replaces the before/after table. Pure CSS transitions on one class.

4.5 Inking

The 8–20 second wait, redesigned
Plate 5 · simulated 7s extraction
  1. Reading northwind.co
  2. Logo found · apple-touch-icon, 180px PNG
  3. Palette #1F4D3A #F2B33D
  4. Type · Fraunces 600
  5. Setting three layouts
Why

A generation takes 8–20 seconds and often calls Firecrawl several times. A spinner that long reads as broken. A log that names what was found turns the wait into evidence: by the time the signature appears, the visitor has already watched Signet find their logo and their exact hex values. That is the "it actually understands my brand" moment, delivered early.

HonestyThe API returns once, so stage timing follows the measured median. The last stage holds, never reaching 100%, until the response lands. No fake percentages.
Slow pathAfter 20s add one line: "Large sites take longer. Still reading." After 45s the route's fallback kit arrives and the log says what was salvaged.
Real valuesWhen the kit lands, the log rewrites its placeholder lines with the real logo source and hex values before the previews ink in.
BuildInkingLog.tsx inside SignatureDemo, replacing the loading spinner. Reuses the Press's ink curve.

First five seconds

The H1, the URL bar, and at least the top edge of the previews must sit above the fold at 1280×720 and 390×844. On mobile, The Press drops below the bar and renders at 100% width, never above the input.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA. Every SVG is one role="img" with a sentence label, not a pile of named shapes. WCAG 2.2.2 requires a way to pause any motion that runs longer than 5s. Every loop stops on focus, off-screen, and under reduced motion, and ends after 3 loops; the Press also gets a small visible pause control.

Mobile

Thumb-reach first: the Sign button is full width at 52px tall under the field. Previews become a horizontal snap-scroll with a "1 / 3" mono counter, not three stacked cards.

Copy

Keep the H1 "Your website is the source of truth." Cut the per-section § 0x eyebrows; DESIGN.md reserves the eyebrow for one deliberate place (the hero). Button labels say what happens: "Sign", "Copy signature", "Email me all 3".

Out of bounds

No scroll-jacking or pinned scroll scenes. The visitor controls the page.
No cursor followers, particles, or parallax. They say "template", not "press".
No gradient, blur, or glass anywhere, including loading states.
No animation that delays the input: the URL bar is interactive at first paint.
No second accent colour. Brand colours inside demos belong to the fictional customer, never to Signet chrome.
No loop without a stop condition: focus, off-screen, reduced motion, or a count.
