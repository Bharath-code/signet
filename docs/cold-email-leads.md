## Sending Notes

- Replace `[link]` with the live app URL, not a shortened one — link shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl) are a major spam trigger
- Replace `[your signature]` with your real Gmail signature made by the tool — it's proof, not decoration; the email body must point at it explicitly (line included in every template above) or the reader won't connect the two
- Validation stage = personal Gmail is fine, no domain needed yet (register one only after paid signal, see `docs/master-plan-validate-first.md`). To avoid spam filtering on a personal account:
  - Send one at a time, manually, spaced a few minutes apart — never all 5+ in one burst
  - One link per email, nothing else
  - Personalize subject line + first line per recipient — identical subject to multiple people is the biggest spam signal on a personal account
  - Plain text, no tracking pixels, no mail-merge tool footers
- Follow-up once at day 5: *"Did the signature come out right when you pasted your URL? Happy to tweak it."* — product-focused, not pushy
